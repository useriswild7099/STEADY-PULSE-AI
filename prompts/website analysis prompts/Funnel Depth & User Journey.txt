# Analysis Frameworks

## 1. The "Next Step" Rule (Linearity)
Visit the Homepage and one Blog Post/Resource page.
- **The Rule:** Every page must have ONE clear logical next step.
- **The Test:**
    - On the **Blog:** Is there a "Bridge CTA" to a related service or lead magnet? Or does it just end (Dead End)?
    - On the **Service Page:** Does it lead to a specific outcome (Book Call), or does it link to 5 other pages (Navigation loop)?

## 2. The "Value Exchange" Audit (Lead Capture)
Identify how the site attempts to capture traffic that isn't ready to buy yet (TOFU/MOFU).
- **The Asset:** Is there a specific Lead Magnet (e.g., "Free Checklist," "Webinar," "Whitepaper")?
- **The Mechanism:** Is it an active Pop-up, an Exit-intent slide-in, or a passive footer form?
- **The Promise:** Is the exchange valuable? ("Join our newsletter" = Low Value vs. "Get the 5-Step System" = High Value).

## 3. The "Thank You" Inference (Post-Conversion)
*Hypothetically* simulate a form fill.
- Does the form imply a redirection to a "Thank You" page (e.g., "You will be redirected...")?
- *Why this matters:* A dedicated Thank You page is the hallmark of a mature funnel (used for tracking pixels and upsells). If the form just says "Message Sent" inline, it's a "Brochure Site."
