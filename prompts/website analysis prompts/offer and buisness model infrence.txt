# Analysis Frameworks

## 1. The CTA "Ticket Size" Signal
Analyze the primary Call to Action (CTA) to determine the likely Average Contract Value (ACV).
* **"Buy Now" / "Add to Cart":** Implies Low-to-Mid Ticket (Transactional).
* **"Start Free Trial":** Implies Product-Led Growth (SaaS/High Volume).
* **"Book a Call" / "Request Demo":** Implies High-Ticket ($2k+) or Complex Sale (Service/Enterprise).
* **"Apply Now":** Implies High-Ticket Coaching or Exclusive Agency (Scarcity/Status play).

## 2. The Leverage Spectrum (Naval Ravikant)
Classify the delivery method:
* **Product/SaaS (Code/Media):** Zero marginal cost of replication. (Look for: "Login", "Instant Access", "Download").
* **Productized Service:** Service with fixed scope/price. (Look for: "Packages", "24-hour turnaround", "Flat rate").
* **Bespoke Agency/Consulting:** Selling time/expertise. (Look for: "Custom Strategy", "Partnership", "Tailored").

## 3. Pricing Psychology & Power
* **Transparent Pricing:** Are prices public? (Indicates confidence in value-to-price ratio or commodity market).
* **Hidden Pricing:** "Contact Us" for price. (Indicates Enterprise value-based pricing or insecurity about high fees).
* **Hormozi Elements:** Look for:
    * **Risk Reversal:** Guarantees ("Money back", "Pay on performance").
    * **Bonuses:** "Free Audit included," "Templates included."
    * **Scarcity/Urgency:** "Only 3 spots left," "Prices go up X."
